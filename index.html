HUB

A personal content tool

HUB is a private tool built and operated by one person. It helps the owner manage their own video content and send finished videos to their own TikTok account as drafts, so they can review and post them in the TikTok app.

What it does

The owner connects their TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit. HUB then uploads videos the owner created to that account's drafts using the TikTok Content Posting API. HUB does not post on behalf of other people and is not offered to the public.
