HUB

A personal content publishing tool

HUB is a private tool built and operated by one individual. It helps the operator prepare their own original short videos and publish them to the TikTok account(s) the operator owns.

What it does

The operator connects their own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit. HUB then uploads the operator's videos through the TikTok Content Posting API, either as drafts for the operator to review in the TikTok app, or as posts where TikTok permits it. Videos made with AI are disclosed as such using TikTok's own AI-generated content setting.

HUB is not offered to the public and does not post on behalf of anyone other than its operator.
