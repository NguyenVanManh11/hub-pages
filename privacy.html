Privacy Policy

HUB · Last updated October 21, 2026

Who this covers

HUB is a private tool used only by its operator. This policy describes what the tool handles when the operator connects their own TikTok account.

What HUB accesses

  • Basic profile information of the connected TikTok account, through the user.info.basic scope.
  • Permission to upload videos to that account's drafts, through the video.upload scope.

What HUB stores

HUB stores the TikTok access and refresh tokens for the connected account. They are encrypted and kept on the operator's own system. Videos are stored on the operator's own system.

Sharing

HUB does not sell, rent or share this data with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok in order to upload the operator's videos.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the operator disconnects the account or deletes them. You can also revoke HUB's access at any time in your TikTok account settings. To ask for data to be deleted, write to the address below.
